Week 03  ·  Assignment 01

Sketch to Story

A hand sketch of a multi-family residential scheme in Oakland, carried into RunDiffusion to generate concept renders that hold the original sketch's geometry — then a courtyard scene and an aerial site plan, each a further pass on the same idea.

Week 04  ·  Assignment 02

Thought Process

Diagram, elevation, and materiality for the same Oakland micro-hub — a FAR 4.0 massing study, a program breakdown across 32,500 sf, and a materials board of corten steel, cast concrete, and glazing set against a hand-drawn elevation.

Week 05  ·  Assignment 04

AI Concept Scenes

One San Jose plaza, tested three ways in Adobe Firefly — a scheme carried straight from sketch, a festive holiday-market variant, and a winter pass laid over the same geometry to see how the architecture reads under different light.

Week 05  ·  Assignment 05

Snowdon Towers

A full SD → CD set for an adaptive mixed-use infill — new residential volumes folded behind a retained masonry storefront, carried from schematic massing through elevations, sections, and a construction-document cover.
